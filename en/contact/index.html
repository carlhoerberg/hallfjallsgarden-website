The seasons change quickly in the mountains, and so does Hållfjället. We remain open through August, September and into October, when the birches turn gold, the air grows crisp and the evenings are once again spent by the fire. In January, we open our doors for winter, when snow returns to the mountains and guests arrive on skis, snowshoes or by snowmobile.
