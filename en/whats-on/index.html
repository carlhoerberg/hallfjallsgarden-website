What’s on

Following the seasons

At Hållfjället, we follow the seasons and let what is happening around us set the tone. Here, we gather the things that belong to the moment — long lunches, days on the mountain, hunting, harvest celebrations and special stays. All according to what the season, the weather and the mountain bring.

Past event · 25–27 Sep 2026

Harvest celebration at Hållfjället

As the gardens reach their most generous and the mountain begins to change colour, we let the harvest set the tone. Five courses, carefully chosen bottles from the cellar and a long autumn evening on the mountain.

New dates are announced as the seasons turn. For reservations and help planning your journey, please email info@hallfjallet.se.
