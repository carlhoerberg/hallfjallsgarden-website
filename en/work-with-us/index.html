A life on the mountain

Hållfjället is a small mountain hotel in roadless country above the village of Ottsjö in the southern Åre mountains. Here, people from around the world gather around long dinners, exceptional ingredients, wood-fired saunas and a longing for something simpler, slower and more present. We grow our own vegetables, work closely with local producers, fish, forage and cook according to the seasons.

We are a small team where everyone helps out and where care for our guests, colleagues and the place we call home is just as important as professional skill. During working weekends we live together on the mountain and become part of the same daily rhythm. It requires responsibility, humility and a willingness to contribute to something larger than yourself.
